7Condensed: product notes + merged screens. Faded options below are superseded.
Model
One account and one session per episode. Every signed-in browser is a screen that follows the script. Each speaker’s device (or interface input) records their own track.
Voice follow
Chrome speech recognition matches spoken words to the script and scrolls word by word. It pauses on ad-libs and suggests lines if it loses its place. Needs internet.
Audio
Mono WAV, 16 or 24-bit, 44.1 or 48 kHz. Saved every 5 s, uploaded in chunks, and synced across devices to one clock. Download as separate files or combined with markers.
Remote
Podstudio records; it does not connect calls. Talk over Zoom, Slack, Teams or Discord with headphones on.
Security
Username, password and TOTP 2FA for every account. HTTPS is needed through Caddy (automatic) or Nginx with Certbot for recording on other devices.
Stack
Astro + Tailwind, WebSocket sync, AudioWorklet PCM capture, OPFS local buffer. Chrome-based browsers only. MIT licensed.

Domain & HTTPS
Chrome only allows microphone access over HTTPS. For recording from other devices, serve Podstudio through a reverse proxy on your own domain.

Reverse proxy
{{ o.label }}
No Certbot needed. Caddy gets and renews certificates on its own. Add two lines to your Caddyfile:
podstudio.example.com { reverse_proxy localhost:4321 }
Nginx needs Certbot to get and renew certificates. Run this once on the server; Certbot sets up automatic renewal.
sudo certbot --nginx -d podstudio.example.comCopy
Without a proxy, only the computer running Podstudio can record. Phones and monitors can still follow the script on your local network.
