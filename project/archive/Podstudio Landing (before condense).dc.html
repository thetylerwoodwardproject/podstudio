REMOTE GUESTS

Every voice gets its own clean track.

In the same room, record everyone on one mic, or on a multichannel interface with one track per input. From anywhere else, each guest records on their own device and Podstudio lines everything up.

NOTEPodstudio records. It doesn’t connect calls. For remote hosts and guests, you still need a way to talk, such as Zoom, Slack, Teams or Discord.

Everyone records locally
Each guest’s browser records their own mic at full quality (16 or 24-bit, 44.1 or 48 kHz), so internet hiccups don’t reach the audio.
Uploaded while you talk
Audio goes to your server in 5-second chunks during the take. If a guest’s connection drops, their browser keeps recording and catches up when it reconnects.
Lined up for you
All tracks share one session clock. Podstudio corrects the small speed differences between devices, so a 60-minute episode stays in sync.
One file per speaker
Download a WAV for each person, all starting at the same moment and the same length, ready to drop into your editor. A rough mix is optional.
Bring your own call
Podstudio isn’t a call service. Talk over Zoom, Slack, Teams, Discord or a phone call while Podstudio records. Remote speakers need headphones so the call doesn’t leak into their mic.
Your server only
Guest audio goes straight to your server, never ours. Guests only see the episodes you invite them to.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Read the docs →
Why Chrome only?+

Voice follow uses Chrome’s speech recognition, and WAV recording relies on audio features Firefox and Safari don’t fully support. Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc all work.

Does voice follow work offline?+

Chrome’s speech recognition sends audio to Google to turn it into text, so the device doing voice follow needs internet. Recording and manual scrolling work offline.

What does OpenAI cost per episode?+

Whisper charges by the minute of audio. A 30-minute episode is usually well under a dollar, including titles, posts and chapters. Check OpenAI’s current pricing for exact rates.

How do retakes show up in the download?+

Your choice. Each take and punch-in can be its own WAV, named after the line it starts on. Or you get one combined WAV with a marker at every take, plus a .csv take list. You can add a short tone between takes to make the joins easy to spot.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?+

Your WAV files, including remote guests’ tracks, go only to your server. If you use transcripts, a compressed copy goes to OpenAI for transcription.

Is Podstudio a call app like Zoom?+

No. Podstudio doesn’t carry audio or video between people. When you record remotely, use Zoom, Slack, Teams, Discord or a phone call to talk. Podstudio records each person’s mic on their own device, separate from the call.

What does a remote guest need?+

A Chrome-based browser, a mic, headphones, your invite link, and a separate call app (Zoom, Slack, Teams, Discord…) so you can hear each other. They run a quick mic check and join. Their audio records on their device and uploads to your server as you go. Keep the tab open until the upload finishes; Podstudio shows when it’s done.

Can guests join without an account?+

Send an invite link. Guests set a password and 2FA, and only see the episodes you share with them.

Does it record video?+

No. Podstudio is audio only, by design.

Run it on your own server.

GOING PUBLIC

Put a reverse proxy in front of it.

To reach Podstudio from outside your network, run it behind Caddy or Nginx on your own domain. Chrome only allows microphone access over HTTPS, so any phone or laptop that records needs a secure address.
