How it would work

  • At export, per person. Each person's edit goes through their EQ, then the compressor, then the optional leveller, before the mix reaches −16 LUFS. The recordings themselves never change, so you can redo the tone any time.
  • Measured from the recording. "Voice before" is that person's long-term average spectrum, taken from their take. The target is a speech curve: a low cut under 80 Hz, a little less mud, a touch of presence.
  • Saved as defaults. Settings → Recording keeps a tone per host, and a guest starts flat or from a preset.
  • A loudness meter for the whole show. Optional (Settings → Recording). Live while you preview the mix, and on the export page after rendering it shows what the finished episode measured: integrated loudness, range and true peak, with its loudness over time.
  • For your DAW. The untouched files are always in the zip; toned copies would be optional, like the levelled ones.
