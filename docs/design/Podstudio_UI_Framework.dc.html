Card rules Surface #141416, radius 14, no border, no shadow. Padding is 20, or 18 top and bottom on a row. Group related settings in one card. Split the rows with a 1px #1c1d20 divider inset 20 from each side. Put a 13/500 grey section label above each card group, with 12 between label and card and 40 between sections. Empty or waiting states use a 1px dashed #2a2b2f outline with no fill. Overlays (sheets, docks) use #141416 with radius 22 on the top edge and a 36×4 grab handle.

Graph rules The plot area is #16171a, a step above the card. The frequency axis is logarithmic from 20 Hz to 20 kHz; the dB axis runs ±24 with a line every 6 dB. Axis labels are mono 10 in text-3. Major grid lines (decades, 1-2-5 steps) are #26272b and minor lines are #1c1d20. The 0 dB line is #3a3b3f. The target curve is always text (#ededea) at 2px. The measured curve takes the channel's speaker color at 2px. The live spectrum is a thin amber line that turns red at the extremes, at 85% opacity, and always sits behind the curves. Channel level uses a 14px vertical bar in the speaker color. Progress bars are amber, 4px tall. Stop is a destructive outline button, never a solid red fill.
