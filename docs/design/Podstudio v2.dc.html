About this episode
Dana Ruiz spent years chasing the perfect microphone. Then she moved out of a closet studio and realised the room had been doing half the work all along.
We talk about the $12 drugstore mic that lasted a decade, why a $400 upgrade can make things sound worse, the three free fixes that beat any new gear, and why every remote guest should be wearing headphones.
