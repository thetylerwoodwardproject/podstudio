Export, step by step
Five steps in processing order: Edit, Noise, Tone, Loudness, Export. Noise now comes before tone, since EQ and compression can't remove it. The preview bar on every step plays 30 s through the chain so far, with a before/after switch for that step. On Noise, "Removed" plays only what's being taken out. Tabs let you go back to any step you've reached.
